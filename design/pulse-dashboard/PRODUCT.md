# Product

## Register

product

## Users

- **WellPeps marketing / community team (primary).** Opens Pulse daily to work through what the community engagement agent drafted: read the original post, check the reply, then approve, edit or skip it. Works in short sessions between other marketing work, usually on a laptop, sometimes on a phone.
- **WellPeps leadership (secondary).** Checks in occasionally for a read on activity, value and risk. Rarely acts; wants to trust that the team and the agent are handling things well.

The job to be done: clear the approval queue quickly and safely, so good, compliant replies reach real conversations while they're still active.

## Product Purpose

Pulse is WellPeps' community engagement agent console. The agent listens to public conversations (Reddit first), triages them, drafts replies in WellPeps' voice that follow WellPeps' compliance rules and point to the free Smart Patient's Guides when a chapter genuinely helps, and holds every reply for a person's approval. The site has three connected areas: **Pulse** (today's summary and the approval queue), **Operator HQ** (WellPeps' agents, posting mode, community permissions, guardrails) and **Knowledge** (the documents the agent follows).

Success: the team clears the queue in minutes, never posts something non-compliant, never misses a safety-relevant post, and leadership can see the value at a glance.

## Brand Personality

Warm, human, conversational. The interface should sound like the replies it produces: friendly, plain-spoken and community-minded, never corporate or robotic. It follows the Pulse Brand Style Guide v1.0 principles: **Human** (approachable language, generous space, clear next step), **Responsive** (activity and progress are legible; the pulse motif is a quiet sign of connection), **Dependable** (bright color anchored by deep navy; navigation, actions and status are predictable).

## Anti-references

- **Generic SaaS analytics dashboard.** Rows of big KPI tiles and charts that don't lead to an action. Numbers are supporting context for the queue, not the headline.

## Design Principles

1. **The queue is the product.** Every screen should make the next approval (or the reason it can't be approved) obvious. Summary and metrics serve that job.
2. **Show the real conversation.** The original post, its community and a one-click path to it on the platform come before the draft, so reviewers judge replies in context.
3. **Safety is visible, never noisy.** Held, escalated and safety-watch states are unmistakable and explained in plain words, without turning the page into an alarm panel.
4. **Talk like a person.** Labels, empty states and errors use the same warm, plain voice as the replies.
5. **Earned trust over decoration.** Calm, familiar product patterns; color and motion only to convey state.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA (Pulse Brand Style Guide §10): 4.5:1 text contrast, 3:1 for control boundaries and meaningful graphics, 44 × 44 px recommended targets, visible keyboard focus never hidden by sticky UI, meaning never conveyed by color alone, underlined links, "Pulse" as logo alt text. Respect reduced-motion preferences. Must work on phones as well as laptops.
