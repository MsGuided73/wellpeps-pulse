## Erectile Dysfunction Is Common—and Often Treatable

*UNDERSTAND THE ISSUE*

Erectile dysfunction, or ED, refers to persistent difficulty achieving or maintaining an erection sufficient for satisfactory sexual activity.

Occasional difficulty is common. Persistent or worsening problems deserve more attention. ED becomes more common with age, but it shouldn’t simply be dismissed as an inevitable part of getting older.

**Possible contributing factors include:**

- **CARDIOVASCULAR HEALTH:** Healthy blood flow is important for erectile function.
- **DIABETES & METABOLIC HEALTH:** Blood-vessel, nerve and hormonal changes may affect sexual function.
- **MEDICATIONS:** Some prescription medications can contribute to erectile difficulties.
- **STRESS & ANXIETY:** Mental and emotional health can influence sexual response.
- **LIFESTYLE:** Sleep, exercise, smoking and alcohol use can all play a role.

## Not Every Sexual Problem Is the Same Problem

*KNOW WHAT YOU’RE TREATING*

Difficulty achieving or maintaining an erection is not necessarily the same as reduced sexual desire.

This distinction matters.

Sildenafil and tadalafil are medications for erectile dysfunction. They improve blood flow to erectile tissue during sexual stimulation. They support the body’s erectile response, but sexual stimulation is still required. They aren’t intended simply to create desire or cause an automatic erection.

Changes in libido may have different contributing factors, including hormones, medications, stress, mental health, relationship factors and other health issues.

That’s another reason treatment should begin with understanding the patient’s actual symptoms and goals.

## How ED Medications Work

*UNDERSTAND THE TREATMENT*

Sildenafil and tadalafil belong to a class of medications called PDE5 inhibitors.

During sexual stimulation, the body uses chemical signals that help relax smooth muscle and increase blood flow to erectile tissue. An enzyme called PDE5 helps break down one of the signaling molecules involved in this process. PDE5 inhibitors reduce that breakdown, helping support blood flow and the natural erectile response during sexual stimulation.

- **WHAT THEY CAN DO:** Support erectile function in appropriate patients.
- **WHAT THEY DON’T DO:** They don’t automatically create sexual desire.
- **STIMULATION STILL MATTERS:** An ED medication doesn’t simply produce an erection on its own. Sexual stimulation is still required.

Sildenafil and tadalafil work through the same general pathway, but differences in duration, dosing and other characteristics can make one approach more appropriate than another for an individual patient.

## Sildenafil, Tadalafil & Daily Tadalafil

*KNOW YOUR OPTIONS*

Two medications can work through the same general pathway without offering the same treatment experience.

**SILDENAFIL**

**An Established As-Needed Choice**

Typically taken before anticipated sexual activity. Sildenafil has a shorter treatment window than tadalafil and is widely used as an as-needed ED medication.

**TADALAFIL — AS NEEDED**

**A Longer Window of Flexibility**

Also taken as needed, but its effects can last considerably longer than sildenafil—up to 36 hours for some patients. That doesn’t mean an erection lasts 36 hours. It refers to the period during which the medication may help support an erectile response when sexual stimulation occurs.

**DAILY TADALAFIL**

**Stay Ready Every Day**

A lower-dose tadalafil regimen taken once daily. Daily dosing can reduce the need to coordinate taking medication with anticipated sexual activity.

## Daily or As Needed?

*PERSONALIZED TREATMENT*

There isn’t one dosing approach that’s automatically better for every patient.

Some patients prefer taking medication only before anticipated sexual activity. Others may prefer a regular daily treatment plan that reduces the need to coordinate medication with a particular occasion.

**A healthcare provider may consider:**

- **Medical History** — Health conditions can affect which treatment may be appropriate.
- **Current Medications** — Potential interactions matter.
- **Frequency & Lifestyle** — How a patient expects to use treatment can affect the choice.
- **Previous Experience** — Effectiveness and tolerability of previous treatments can be relevant.
- **Patient Preference** — Convenience, flexibility and spontaneity can all matter.

The longest-lasting or highest-dose option isn’t automatically the best choice.

## One Interaction Every Patient Should Know About

*KNOW THE INTERACTIONS*

There is an important safety issue associated with common PDE5 inhibitors such as sildenafil and tadalafil.

> **Red Flag**
>
> Nitrate medications
>
> Sildenafil and tadalafil should not be taken with nitrate medications because the combination can cause a dangerous drop in blood pressure.
>
> That’s one reason accurately reporting your medications matters.
>
> Patients should also tell their healthcare provider about other medications, supplements and medical conditions because additional interactions and precautions may affect whether treatment is appropriate.
>
> And if emergency medical care is needed, healthcare professionals should know about recent ED-medication use.

> **Smart Patient Insight**
>
> Never hide medications or health conditions because you’re concerned they might prevent you from getting a prescription. That information helps keep treatment safe.

## Side Effects Are Part of the Conversation

*KNOW WHAT TO WATCH FOR*

Every prescription medication has potential risks and side effects.

Commonly reported effects with sildenafil or tadalafil can include:

- **HEADACHE**
- **FACIAL FLUSHING**
- **NASAL CONGESTION**
- **INDIGESTION**
- **DIZZINESS**

Tadalafil may also be associated with back pain or muscle aches. Sildenafil can cause temporary visual changes in some patients. Patients should contact an appropriate healthcare professional about persistent, worsening or concerning symptoms.

> **Red Flag**
>
> Certain symptoms require more urgent attention. Seek immediate medical evaluation for circumstances such as:
>
> Chest pain • Sudden vision loss • Sudden hearing loss • An erection lasting more than four hours • Signs of a serious allergic reaction

## What If the Medication Doesn’t Work as Expected?

*WHEN TREATMENT NEEDS ADJUSTMENT*

Sometimes the first treatment doesn’t produce the experience a patient expected. That doesn’t mean the answer is:

**“Take more.”**

Patients should not independently increase their dose, change their dosing schedule or combine prescription treatments. Instead, discuss the experience with a healthcare provider. The provider may consider:

- **HOW IT WAS USED:** Were the medication instructions followed?
- **TIMING:** Was it taken at an appropriate time?
- **FOOD OR ALCOHOL:** Could either have affected the treatment experience?
- **OTHER MEDICATIONS:** Could an interaction be relevant?
- **HEALTH FACTORS:** Could another issue be contributing?
- **TREATMENT APPROACH:** Would another dose, schedule or medication be appropriate?

## Sexual Wellness & Your Overall Health

*LOOK AT THE WHOLE PICTURE*

Medication can play an important role in treating ED, but sexual function doesn’t exist separately from the rest of your health.

- **CARDIOVASCULAR HEALTH:** Healthy circulation is fundamental to erectile function.
- **METABOLIC HEALTH:** Diabetes, obesity and other metabolic conditions can contribute to ED.
- **PHYSICAL ACTIVITY:** Regular activity supports cardiovascular health and circulation.
- **SLEEP:** Consistent, quality sleep supports overall health.
- **MENTAL WELL-BEING:** Stress, anxiety, depression and relationship concerns can influence sexual function and satisfaction.
- **SMOKING & ALCOHOL:** Smoking can impair circulation, while excessive alcohol can negatively affect sexual performance.

These aren’t substitutes for appropriate medical treatment. They are reminders that addressing overall health can be an important part of sexual wellness.
