# 13. Current Claims, Disclaimers and Prohibited Claims

## 13.1 Approved Core Claims / Qualifiers

- Treatment is provider-guided and prescribed only when medically appropriate.
- Individual results vary.
- No specific outcome can be guaranteed.
- Medication is included if prescribed according to the selected treatment program.
- Some WellPeps therapies use compounded medications prepared by licensed compounding pharmacies when appropriate.
- Compounded medications are not FDA-approved as finished drug products.
- Representatives may provide general educational information but not individualized medical advice.

## 13.2 Current General Legal / Platform Disclaimer

“WellPeps is a platform that connects patients with licensed healthcare providers and pharmacy partners. Medical services are provided by independent providers. WellPeps does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.”

## 13.3 Wellness-Therapy Disclaimer Found in Existing Creative

“These statements have not been evaluated by the FDA. Treatments are not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease.”

CONFIRM: Determine where this disclaimer is legally/operationally required and approve the final exact language before community personnel reuse it.

## 13.4 Prohibited / Unapproved Language

- Guaranteed / guaranteed results
- Everyone qualifies
- Completely safe / no risk / no side effects
- You should take... / you need... / stop taking...
- This is definitely caused by...
- Better, safer or more effective than a competitor unless specifically approved and substantiated
- Any promise of specific weight loss, hair restoration, sexual-health improvement or other clinical outcome
