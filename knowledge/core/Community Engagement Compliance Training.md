*Community Engagement Compliance Training*

Required Training for Employees, Contractors, Community Representatives, Influencers, Brand Ambassadors and Other Authorized Representatives

Version 1.0 | August 2026 | Internal Training

# Training Overview

This training teaches the compliance boundaries that apply when an authorized representative communicates publicly about WellPeps, its services, related wellness topics, or treatment categories. It is designed to be completed together with the WellPeps Community Engagement Policy and Community Engagement Operations & Training Manual.

> Training Objective After completing this training, a representative should be able to recognize what can be answered, what must not be answered, when affiliation must be disclosed, how to protect privacy, how to handle sponsored content and testimonials, and when an issue must be escalated.

## Who Should Complete This Training

- WellPeps employees and MSO personnel who engage publicly
- Contractors and community engagement personnel
- Marketing or public relations personnel authorized to respond publicly
- Influencers and Brand Ambassadors
- Affiliate partners and other authorized representatives

Different roles have different permissions. Influencers and Brand Ambassadors operate in a narrower lane than trained WellPeps community personnel. Licensed clinicians require separate clinical guidance for public participation.

## How to Use This Training

1. Read Modules 1-10.
2. Review the scenario examples.
3. Complete the Knowledge Check and Scenario Assessment.
4. Review any incorrect answers with a supervisor or designated trainer.
5. Do not participate independently until WellPeps has approved the individual for the applicable role.

> Important Scope Note This training is based on WellPeps' supplied Community Engagement Policy, Community Engagement Operations Manual, Community Groups and Forums strategy, and the expanded Operations & Training Manual. It is operational compliance training, not legal advice or a substitute for role-specific clinical, legal, privacy, or regulatory guidance.

# Module 1. The WellPeps Community Engagement Standard

## Learning Objectives

- Understand the purpose of community engagement
- Apply the 'educate before you promote' principle
- Recognize why transparency is required

WellPeps' objective is to become a trusted educational resource, not simply another advertiser. Representatives should provide accurate, transparent, respectful information while protecting privacy and staying within approved messaging.

## The Core Principles

- Be transparent about your affiliation.
- Educate before you promote.
- Be respectful, helpful, and professional.
- Never provide individualized medical advice.
- Protect privacy.
- Use approved information and claims.
- Follow platform and community rules.
- When uncertain, do not guess - escalate.

## The 80/20 Principle

As an operating guideline, community participation should generally favor education and useful information over promotion. Approximately 80% of activity should be educational/value-oriented and approximately 20% may be promotional when the platform or community permits it. If a community prohibits promotion, the permitted promotional percentage is zero.

> Compliance Test Ask: Would this response still provide value if the promotional element were removed? If not, reconsider whether you should post it.

# Module 2. Transparency, Affiliation and Authentic Participation

## Why Disclosure Matters

A WellPeps representative must not appear to be an independent consumer when they are actually promoting or discussing WellPeps on the company's behalf. Undisclosed or fake grassroots participation conflicts with WellPeps policy and can undermine trust.

## Approved Affiliation Language

- “I work with WellPeps.”
- “I’m part of the WellPeps team.”
- “I’m a WellPeps Brand Ambassador.”
- “I partner with WellPeps.”

## Anonymous Promotion Is Prohibited

Do not create anonymous, fake, or undisclosed accounts to praise WellPeps, defend WellPeps, recommend WellPeps, steer discussions toward WellPeps, or make company participation appear organic.

### Scenario 2A - Reddit

| Situation | An employee finds a Reddit thread asking for recommendations for online weight-management clinics. The employee has a personal Reddit account that does not identify their employer. |
|---|---|
| Correct action | The employee may participate only if the subreddit rules permit it and the WellPeps affiliation is clearly disclosed. The employee must not pose as an unaffiliated customer. |
| Why | WellPeps requires transparent affiliation and prohibits anonymous promotional behavior. |

### Scenario 2B - Helpful Answer Without Promotion

| Situation | A group member asks generally how telehealth clinics evaluate patients. The question does not mention WellPeps. |
|---|---|
| Correct action | A trained representative may provide a useful general answer if group rules permit participation. If WellPeps is discussed or linked, disclose the relationship. |
| Why | Education can build trust without forcing a WellPeps promotion into every conversation. |

# Module 3. Medical Advice and Clinical Boundaries

## The Most Important Boundary

Community representatives do not diagnose, prescribe, recommend individualized treatment, interpret laboratory results, or tell people to change medications. Treatment decisions require evaluation by a licensed healthcare provider.

## Generally Permitted

- General wellness education
- General information about telehealth
- How the WellPeps assessment/provider-review process works
- General treatment-category education using approved materials
- Publicly available WellPeps educational information

## Not Permitted

- Diagnosing a condition
- Recommending a specific medication to an individual
- Recommending a dosage
- Interpreting laboratory results
- Advising someone to start, stop, increase, decrease, or substitute medication
- Determining whether a specific individual qualifies
- Guaranteeing a treatment outcome

## The General-to-Individual Boundary

A question can begin as general education and become individualized. For example, “What is the difference between semaglutide and tirzepatide?” can be answered generally using approved information. “Which one should I take given my blood pressure and medications?” requires individualized clinical judgment and must be referred to a licensed provider.

> Approved Boundary Pattern “That’s something a licensed healthcare provider would need to evaluate based on your individual medical history. I can provide general educational information, but I can’t recommend a treatment for you personally.”

### Scenario 3A - Medication Selection

| Situation | A user gives their height, weight and medical history and asks whether tirzepatide would be better for them than semaglutide. |
|---|---|
| Correct action | Do not recommend either medication. Explain that a licensed provider must determine what is appropriate after reviewing the person's medical history. General differences may be explained using approved information. |
| Why | The question requires individualized treatment selection. |

### Scenario 3B - Dosing

| Situation | A follower asks an ambassador, “What dose do you take? Should I start at that dose too?” |
|---|---|
| Correct action | The ambassador may truthfully discuss an approved personal experience if permitted, but must not recommend that the follower use the same dose. Direct the follower to a licensed provider. |
| Why | A person's own experience does not authorize individualized dosing advice to another person. |

# Module 4. Claims, Results and Comparisons

## No Guaranteed Outcomes

- Do not promise a specific amount of weight loss.
- Do not promise hair restoration.
- Do not promise sexual-health improvement.
- Do not guarantee wellness or clinical results.
- Do not imply that everyone will respond similarly.

## Safety and Effectiveness Language

Do not make blanket statements such as “this is completely safe,” “this always works,” or “there are no side effects.” Approved company materials and licensed providers should guide treatment-related information.

## Comparative Claims

Do not claim that WellPeps, a medication, a treatment, or a service is better, safer, more effective, or cheaper than another option unless the specific comparison has been approved and is supported by current information.

### Scenario 4A - Results

| Situation | A commenter asks, “How much weight will I lose in three months?” |
|---|---|
| Correct action | State that individual results vary and no specific outcome can be guaranteed. Do not supply an expected number unless WellPeps has specifically approved substantiated language for that use. |
| Why | The existing WellPeps policy prohibits promises of specific weight-loss outcomes. |

### Scenario 4B - Competitor Comparison

| Situation | A user asks whether WellPeps is cheaper and better than a named competitor. |
|---|---|
| Correct action | Explain current WellPeps pricing and what is included using approved information. Do not make unverified claims about the competitor or claim WellPeps is clinically better. |
| Why | Comparative claims must be approved and supported. |

# Module 5. Privacy and HIPAA Boundaries

## Public Forums Are Not Appropriate for Personal Medical Information

Representatives must protect privacy and must not invite people to post sensitive health or account information publicly.

## Never Request Publicly

- Medical records
- Laboratory reports
- Prescription information
- Dates of birth
- Insurance information
- Photographs intended for clinical evaluation
- Other protected or sensitive health information

## If Someone Voluntarily Posts Medical Information

Do not repeat, quote, analyze, or amplify the information. Encourage the person not to continue posting personal medical details and route appropriate support to an approved private channel.

## Do Not Confirm Patient Status

Do not publicly confirm whether someone is or is not a WellPeps patient without appropriate authorization.

## DMs Do Not Change the Clinical Boundary

A direct message may be appropriate for customer-service routing or directing someone to an approved care channel. It is not permission for a community representative to interpret labs, review medical records, diagnose symptoms, or recommend treatment.

### Scenario 5A - Lab Results in Comments

| Situation | A person posts a screenshot of laboratory results and asks WellPeps to interpret them. |
|---|---|
| Correct action | Do not interpret the results. Ask the person not to post personal medical information publicly and direct them to the appropriate provider/care channel. |
| Why | Laboratory interpretation is clinical, and the public post raises privacy concerns. |

### Scenario 5B - Existing Patient Complaint

| Situation | A commenter says they are a patient and posts their full name, medication and account issue. |
|---|---|
| Correct action | Do not repeat the details or confirm patient status. Acknowledge the concern, tell them not to post medical/account details publicly, and route the matter through the approved support process. |
| Why | Public complaint handling must protect privacy. |

# Module 6. FTC Endorsements, Influencers and Testimonials

## Sponsored Relationships Must Be Disclosed

Influencers, Brand Ambassadors, affiliates, and other compensated or sponsored representatives must clearly disclose the relationship. The WellPeps source policy requires appropriate FTC endorsement disclosures such as #ad, #sponsored, or equivalent platform-specific disclosure.

## Disclosure Principles

- The disclosure should be clear and easy for the audience to notice.
- Do not rely only on a profile disclosure when the relationship should be apparent in the content itself.
- Do not hide the disclosure in a long group of hashtags or other text where it may be missed.
- Use WellPeps-approved disclosure language and any required platform tools.

## Testimonials

- Must be truthful.
- Must reflect actual experiences.
- Must avoid misleading implications.
- Must not guarantee results.
- Must not imply that personal results are typical unless properly substantiated and accompanied by approved disclosures.

## Influencer Medical Questions

Influencers should generally direct medical questions to WellPeps or a licensed healthcare provider rather than answering them. They may not turn their personal experience into a treatment recommendation for a follower.

### Scenario 6A - Personal Experience

| Situation | An influencer had a positive WellPeps experience and wants to say, “This will work for you too.” |
|---|---|
| Correct action | The influencer may describe a truthful approved personal experience with proper sponsorship disclosure, but must remove the promise that another person will achieve the same result. |
| Why | Testimonials cannot guarantee or misleadingly imply results for others. |

### Scenario 6B - Comment Question

| Situation | A follower asks a sponsored creator whether they should switch from another medication to the one the creator uses. |
|---|---|
| Correct action | The creator should not advise the switch. Use approved referral language directing the follower to a licensed healthcare provider. |
| Why | Medication changes require clinical judgment. |

# Module 7. Complaints, Negative Reviews and Misinformation

## Complaints

1. Acknowledge the concern.
2. Do not argue or tell the person their experience is wrong.
3. Do not request medical or account details publicly.
4. Move appropriate customer-service routing to an approved private channel.
5. Escalate internally when required.
6. Document the interaction when required by the operating procedure.

## Negative Comments

Legitimate criticism should not be removed merely because it is unfavorable. Moderation should follow platform policy and WellPeps rules.

## Misinformation

Correct misinformation respectfully when you have approved, reliable information and the correction is useful. Correct the information, not the person. Do not become argumentative.

### Scenario 7A - Angry Complaint

| Situation | A user posts, “WellPeps ripped me off. Nobody should use them.” |
|---|---|
| Correct action | Acknowledge the concern, disclose affiliation if needed, avoid debating facts in public, protect privacy, and route the issue to the appropriate support process. |
| Why | Public arguments can worsen reputational risk and may expose private information. |

### Scenario 7B - False Pricing Claim

| Situation | A group member posts an incorrect WellPeps price. |
|---|---|
| Correct action | A trained representative may politely correct the price using current approved information, disclose affiliation, and provide the official approved resource if useful. |
| Why | Factual correction is permitted when accurate, transparent and non-combative. |

# Module 8. Adverse Events, Medical Emergencies and Escalation

## Adverse Events

The WellPeps source materials require immediate escalation when adverse events are reported. Community representatives should not investigate clinically, determine causation, dismiss the report, or troubleshoot treatment publicly.

- Preserve the relevant communication according to the approved process.
- Do not state that the medication caused or did not cause the event.
- Do not recommend medication changes.
- Escalate immediately using the designated WellPeps safety/clinical procedure.

## Medical Emergencies

Do not attempt to manage a medical emergency through social media. Advise the individual to seek immediate emergency medical care and follow the designated escalation procedure.

## Other Immediate Escalations

- Potential HIPAA/privacy concern
- Legal threat or attorney contact
- Media inquiry
- Regulatory agency contact
- Threats or serious safety concerns
- Potential disclosure of confidential company information

> Implementation Note The current WellPeps materials establish which issues must be escalated but do not yet identify the named internal contacts, response-time requirements, or after-hours procedure. Those details must be added to the live training before deployment.

### Scenario 8A - Severe Symptoms

| Situation | A TikTok commenter says they developed severe symptoms after taking medication obtained through the service. |
|---|---|
| Correct action | Do not diagnose, minimize, or determine causation. Direct the person to appropriate immediate medical care based on the approved emergency language and escalate the report immediately under the WellPeps safety procedure. |
| Why | This is both a clinical boundary and an escalation issue. |

### Scenario 8B - Reporter Inquiry

| Situation | A journalist DMs a community representative asking for a statement about WellPeps. |
|---|---|
| Correct action | Do not provide substantive comment. Preserve the inquiry and refer it to the designated WellPeps media/management contact. |
| Why | Only authorized spokespersons should respond to media inquiries. |

# Module 9. Platform and Community Compliance

## Facebook Groups

- Follow each group's rules.
- Obtain administrator permission where required.
- Contribute value before promotion.
- Do not appear only when promoting WellPeps.
- Do not use unsolicited DMs to pressure members.

## Reddit

- Disclose affiliation whenever discussing WellPeps.
- Do not use fake or anonymous promotional accounts.
- Prioritize useful answers over links.
- Respect subreddit rules and moderator decisions.
- Avoid repetitive promotion, coordinated praise, or other behavior that appears manipulative.

## Instagram and TikTok

- Use approved treatment-related messaging.
- Use required sponsorship disclosures.
- Keep public responses educational.
- Redirect individualized medical questions.
- Escalate safety/adverse-event comments.

## LinkedIn

- Maintain a professional tone.
- Use approved educational content and company information.
- Avoid patient-specific matters.
- Do not improvise clinical claims.

> Platform Rule WellPeps approval does not override a platform, group, subreddit, or community rule. If the community does not permit the activity, do not do it.

# Module 10. Content Approval and Approved Messaging

## Use Current Approved Information

Representatives should rely on approved WellPeps materials for company descriptions, services, pricing, treatment-category education, disclosures, claims, and links.

## Do Not Create New Healthcare Claims

The Community Engagement Policy specifically prohibits representatives from modifying approved medical claims or creating new healthcare claims without authorization.

## Pre-Approval

Unless specifically authorized otherwise, public marketing, educational, influencer, ambassador, and promotional content referencing WellPeps must receive approval before publication.

## Real-Time Community Responses

The expanded Operations Manual anticipates that trained personnel may eventually be authorized to handle defined categories of routine responses without individual pre-approval. WellPeps has not yet finalized those approval levels. Until that authority is formally established, representatives should follow the current approval requirements.

> Next Supporting Document A separate WellPeps Approved Messaging & Response Guide should provide the current company descriptions, program language, pricing language, disclosures, FAQ responses, links, complaint language, and approved response patterns used during daily engagement.

## Scenario Assessment - Expected Principles

1. May answer generally if group rules permit; disclose affiliation when appropriate; no escalation ordinarily required.

2. Do not advise stopping medication. Refer to a licensed provider. Escalate only if other safety/adverse-event facts require it.

3. Protect privacy; do not repeat the prescription information; route the billing complaint to approved support; consider privacy escalation depending on circumstances.

4. The influencer may describe truthful personal experience but must not state or imply that results are guaranteed or typical without approved substantiation/disclosures.

5. Do not assess causation or advise treatment changes. Use approved emergency/provider language as appropriate and immediately escalate under the safety procedure.

6. Explain current approved WellPeps information; do not make unapproved superiority or competitor claims.

7. Do not provide substantive media comment; escalate to the designated media/management contact.

8. Respect moderator rules and stop or adjust participation. Do not argue. Escalate internally if needed for account/community strategy.

9. General education may be provided using approved materials; do not convert it into a personal recommendation.

10. Do not diagnose from a photograph or request additional clinical images through community messaging; refer to an appropriate licensed provider/care channel.
